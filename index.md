# Welcome to the **CLIMATE Research Group**

The CLIMATE Research Group focuses on developing next‑generation technologies for **CO₂ utilisation**, **chemical looping**, **industrial decarbonisation**, and **sustainable fuel generation**. Our work spans advanced materials synthesis, multi‑scale reaction engineering, and computational modelling of high‑temperature thermochemical systems.

We aim to accelerate the transition toward a low‑carbon future by combining **thermochemical innovation**, **reactor engineering**, and **computational simulation**.

---

## 🔬 Research Areas

### **Chemical Looping**
Chemical looping enables efficient fuel conversion, CO₂ separation, and oxygen delivery without direct air–fuel contact. We develop novel oxygen carriers, study redox kinetics, and design looping reactors for hydrogen production, syngas generation, and CO₂ utilisation.

### **CO₂ Capture & Utilisation**
We investigate thermochemical pathways that convert CO₂ into valuable products such as syngas, methanol, carbonates, and solid carbon. Our work integrates sorbent development, looping‑based capture systems, and catalytic materials for CO₂ conversion.

### **Industrial Decarbonisation**
We design process‑intensified solutions for hard‑to‑abate sectors such as steel, cement, and chemicals. Our research includes techno‑economic analysis, lifecycle assessment, and integration of low‑carbon technologies into existing industrial systems.

### **Materials for CO₂ Utilisation**
We synthesise advanced oxygen carriers, catalysts, and sorbents tailored for CO₂ conversion and looping processes. Material properties are linked to reactor performance through kinetic studies and multi‑scale modelling.

### **Kinetics & Reaction Engineering**
We perform TGA experiments, fixed‑bed reactor studies, and micro‑kinetic modelling to quantify reaction rates, activation energies, and transport limitations. These insights guide reactor design and process optimisation.

### **CFD of Chemical Looping Processes**
We use CFD to simulate multiphase flow, heat transfer, and reaction kinetics in chemical looping reactors. Our models help optimise reactor geometry, operating strategies, and scale‑up pathways.

### **Sustainable Fuel Generation**
We explore thermochemical routes for producing hydrogen, syngas, and carbon‑neutral fuels using looping processes and CO₂ conversion. Our work integrates renewable energy inputs and catalytic upgrading.
